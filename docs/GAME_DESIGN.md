# Glitter Merge — Game Design

## Identity

Glitter Merge is a mobile-first merge game and luxury LGBTQ+ world-building experience.

**Identity:** GAY. RICH. LUX. FABULOUS.

**Player fantasy:** Build the most fabulous LGBTQ+ luxury destination imaginable.

Fairyland Merge is the **structural muse** — the kind of merge game Glitter Merge is. It is not the skin, story, or brand.

We take from that genre:

- You merge **inside a living destination**, not on a spreadsheet
- **3** identical items become **1** upgrade; **5** become **2**
- Energy paces harvest, not the joy of merging
- Quests give the next merge a reason
- The world comes alive as you discover looks
- Long-term joy is building and expanding a place that feels like yours
- Calm session length, no high score, no rush

We never take:

- Fairies, dragons, unicorns, elves, mist, medieval kingdoms
- Enchanted forests, fantasy prophecy, or their characters
- Their art, UI chrome, names, or marketing
- A dark, muddy, or generic-fantasy look

This is **not** a fantasy/fairy game with LGBTQ+ characters. There are no fairies, enchanted forests, medieval kingdoms, mystical prophecy, generic fantasy villages, or dark-fantasy aesthetics.

Glitter's translation of that structure:

| Fairyland-style structure | Glitter City |
| --- | --- |
| Merge island | Glitter Neighborhood terrace |
| Fog / locked land | Velvet-rope lots and unopened districts |
| Quests | City brief ("Tonight") |
| Fairy homes / buildings | Venues that light up from discoveries |
| Creatures and flowers | Luxury looks — beauty, fashion, jewels, nightlife |
| Expand the kingdom | Build the most fabulous LGBTQ+ destination |

The fantasy is luxury, wealth, fashion, nightlife, real estate, entertainment, beauty, travel, friendship, celebration, and fabulous LGBTQ+ culture.

The world should feel colorful, glossy, bright, glamorous, playful, aspirational, expensive, energetic, and unmistakably queer. Avoid muddy, gloomy, muted, or depressing visuals.

## Core loop

`MERGE → DISCOVER → EARN → BUILD → UPGRADE → UNLOCK → EXPAND → MERGE`

- The **merge board** is the primary gameplay engine.
- The **city/world** is the long-term progression reward.
- **Characters** provide personality and story (not built in Phase 0).
- **Collections** provide discovery and completion goals (model only in Phase 0).
- **Events** provide recurring content (not built in Phase 0).

## Setting

The player begins in a modest corner of **Glitter City**: the Glitter Neighborhood.

Later city progression may include Rainbow Heights, the Fashion District, the Nightlife District, Luxury Beach, Downtown Glitter, Billionaire's Row, and future districts. Those areas are catalogued, not simulated, in Phase 0.

## Time economy

**Time is the ultimate luxury.**

Future timers may cover production, construction, upgrades, special activities, and events. Players may wait, earn reductions, use boosts, or spend premium currency to accelerate. Phase 0 only establishes the timer interfaces.

## Economy (interfaces only)

| Currency | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Glitter Cash | Primary soft currency |
| Glitter Gems | Premium currency |
| Energy | Action pacing |
| Event currencies | Temporary, future events |

Phase 0 does not build shops, ads, VIP, or monetization UI.

## Merge families (initial data)

Six five-tier families ship as data. The merge engine never names them.

- **Beauty:** Lip Balm → Lip Gloss → Luxury Gloss → Diamond Gloss → Couture Gloss
- **Fashion:** Basic Shirt → Cute Outfit → Glam Outfit → Runway Look → Couture Look
- **Jewelry:** Beads → Bracelet → Gold Bracelet → Diamond Bracelet → Statement Jewelry
- **Real Estate:** Studio → Condo → Luxury Condo → Penthouse → Mansion
- **Nightlife:** Bar Cart → Bar → Lounge → Club → Mega Club
- **Automobiles:** Compact → Coupe → Luxury Car → Sports Car → Supercar

## Merge rules (Phase 0)

- One board cell can stage a stack of identical items.
- Drag or tap-select, then drop onto a matching item to stack.
- Drop onto an empty cell to move.
- **3** identical items merge into **1** next-tier item.
- **5** identical items merge into **2** next-tier items.
- Higher counts spend as many 5-merges as possible, then 3-merges.
- Max-tier items cannot merge.

Special item mechanics are reserved and unimplemented.
